# Compliance Map: bounty brief

Copied from the Tiny Bounties board (tinybounties.com, Bounty board) on 12 Nov 2026.

Launch bounty, TinySet + TinyResearch. Hard. API / Agentic App. **Use TinyResearch.**
$75 gift card and 300 pts for the one winner; 75 pts for every other valid entry.
Open until Tue 17 Nov 2026 (11:59 PM PT). Individual entries. Top 3 builds approved.
Each entry must be original work built for this bounty and needs its own LinkedIn post.

> Where should I set up? Opening a café, registering a company or running a drone business takes
> very different paperwork depending on the place. Build a TinyFish tool that takes one activity and
> compares at least three places side by side: every licence, filing, cost and time to approval,
> pulled live from official government sites, with a recommendation for where it's easiest to start.

What they're looking for: the comparison a founder would use to decide where to start, then hand to
a lawyer to confirm.

## Submission requirements

1. Takes an activity and at least three places (cities, states or countries) as input
2. Pulls the requirements for each place from official government sources
3. Returns a side-by-side comparison: steps, total cost, time to approval, renewals and ongoing
   filings, with a source link for every item
4. Gives a recommendation and explains the trade-offs
5. Demo on at least two different activities
6. Uses TinyResearch, with a short explanation of how it is used (Search, Fetch and Agent can help
   around it)

## Approval criteria

1. Every requirement links to the official page it came from, with the date it was checked
2. Comparable: the same kinds of requirements are lined up across every place
3. Works for different activities and places, not a hardcoded few
4. Gaps and unclear rules are flagged, never guessed
5. Not checking whether a person or business holds a licence (that's TinyVerify)

## How it's judged (weighted most, in order)

1. Comparability: the same requirements lined up cleanly across every place
2. Accuracy: every item matches its official source, with a link and a checked date
3. Decision value: the recommendation and trade-offs actually help someone choose
4. Coverage: works across many activities and places
5. Honesty: unclear or missing rules are flagged, never guessed

## Endpoint scoring

1 endpoint 50 pts, 2 = 100, 3 = 150, 4+ = 200. Endpoints must contribute meaningfully; padding
usage to reach a higher tier does not qualify.
